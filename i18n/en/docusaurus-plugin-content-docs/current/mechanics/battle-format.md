## Two slots, one plan

All 27 encounters use `GEN_9_DOUBLES`. Version 2.0 adds 56 opening actions in total, including Fake Out, Tailwind, stat setup and weather combinations.

## What scripts do

Each trainer shares a short sequence across both slots. It is not a complete turn-by-turn plan: there are no fixed targets, conditions or loops, and Fake Out does not automatically repeat after switching. Protect, immunity, Taunt, weather and other normal rules can make a move fail.

## Mega, Tera and Z-Moves

Teams contain items and settings for these mechanics and depend on Mega Showdown and ZA Mega. Activation depends on RCT; all mechanics are not guaranteed in the same fight. The inspected RCT prevents Tera after Mega for the same trainer.
